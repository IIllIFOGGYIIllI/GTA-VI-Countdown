Privacy Policy.

LAST UPDATED: 15 NOVEMBER 2026

Vice City Countdown is an independently operated fan website and installable progressive web app (PWA). We aim to keep it simple and privacy-conscious.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your chosen timezone in your browser's local storage so your countdown remains correct on return visits. A service worker stores website files (such as HTML, CSS, icons and scripts) in the browser cache for offline use. Removing website data in your browser clears these preferences and cached files.

Data collection and tracking

This version of the website has no accounts, comments, advertising systems, visitor analytics, third-party tracking scripts or marketing cookies. We do not intentionally collect names, emails or precise locations through the site. Automatic timezone detection uses your browser's local timezone setting; it does not access GPS.

Hosting and external services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, your internet provider and your browser may process technical access data, such as IP addresses and request logs, under their own policies. Read GitHub's privacy statement.

When you choose to reveal the official screenshot gallery, your browser requests selected images from Rockstar Games' servers, which may receive technical connection information such as an IP address or browser details. Those images are not requested until you deliberately reveal the media section. When you open an official Rockstar source or other external link, you leave this website. The destination may use cookies or collect data under its own policies. We do not embed those sites' trackers here.

Contact and changes

To report a privacy concern, open an issue in the project's GitHub repository. Please do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue. This notice will be updated if the app's data practices change.
