Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: 16 NOVEMBER 2026

Vice City Countdown is an independently operated fan website and installable progressive web app (PWA). We aim to keep it simple and privacy-conscious.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your chosen timezone, launch checklist progress, and optional platform, motion, sound and strict-spoiler preferences in your browser's local storage so your experience remains consistent on return visits. These settings are not sent to the project owner. Launch postcard images are drawn locally in your browser and downloaded by you, and calendar reminders are generated locally as .ics files. A service worker stores website files (such as HTML, CSS, icons and scripts) in the browser cache for offline use. Removing website data in your browser clears these preferences, checklist progress and cached files.

Data collection and tracking

This version of the website has no accounts, comments, advertising systems, visitor analytics, third-party tracking scripts or marketing cookies. News headlines and the optional Rockstar-confirmed information archive come from editor-reviewed files in the public repository. The character/story archive is fetched only after a visitor selects Show Story Details; The optional archive is not pre-cached on installation; it may only be saved in the browser cache after someone chooses to reveal it. Automatic Newswire checks run through GitHub Actions rather than through a visitor's browser. We do not intentionally collect names, emails or precise locations through the site. Automatic timezone detection uses your browser's local timezone setting; it does not access GPS.

Hosting and external services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, your internet provider and your browser may process technical access data, such as IP addresses and request logs, under their own policies. Read GitHub's privacy statement.

The public Newswire uses approved scenic screenshots loaded from Rockstar Games' servers as thumbnails when they enter view, except when Strict Spoiler Lock is enabled. The character, story and location thumbnails are only requested after you choose Show Story Details, and the full screenshot gallery still requires its own deliberate reveal. Rockstar Games may receive technical connection information such as an IP address or browser details when these images load. Images are not uploaded to this fan site and are not packaged for offline use. When you open an official Rockstar source or other external link, you leave this website. The destination may use cookies or collect data under its own policies. We do not embed those sites' trackers here.

Contact and changes

To report a privacy concern, open an issue in the project's GitHub repository. Please do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue. This notice will be updated if the app's data practices change.
