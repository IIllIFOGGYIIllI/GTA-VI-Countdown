CHAPTER ONE / AN ORIGINAL SPOILER-FREE INTRODUCTION

Just Like Paradise

The beginning of a journey through Leonida

Leonida is a place of endless sunshine, open roads and opportunities that always seem just within reach. But behind the postcard-perfect beaches and glowing city lights, life has a way of becoming complicated.

For Jason and Lucia, this is where the journey begins. The world feels enormous, the future uncertain, and every new day brings another reason to wonder what lies around the next corner.

In a place where everybody is chasing something, staying out of trouble might prove harder than it sounds.

Editorial note: this is a mood-setting introduction inspired by GTA VI's official setting and protagonists, not a factual recap of missions or a walkthrough. There are no story spoilers here.
