The Lowdown

Does the countdown automatically detect my timezone?

Yes. The browser's timezone is selected by default, and you can change it to another region. Your selection is saved on this device only.

Does the countdown use my exact console unlock time?

It uses Rockstar's published midnight-local release schedule. Your PlayStation or Xbox account region may differ from your current location, so choose the corresponding region and verify with your console store.

Will this site show GTA VI story spoilers or leaks?

We do not publish leaks, plot details, story reveals, datamined content or unreviewed auto-imported articles. External official links may contain media or additional information, so open them at your discretion.

Does the PWA need internet access?

The timer and installed interface work offline after the first visit. Official linked news pages require an internet connection.

Is this an official Rockstar Games website?

No. This is a free, unofficial fan-made countdown, not endorsed by or affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
