Spoiler Policy

Last Updated: 22 November 2026

Our priority is to protect the experience of discovering Grand Theft Auto VI for yourself. The site is spoiler-conscious by design, rather than an uncontrolled live feed.

What we do not publish

  • Leaks, datamined material, stolen footage, early gameplay uploads or rumours.
  • Unannounced story beats, missions, endings, character twists or surprise reveals. The separate optional guide contains only Rockstar's published pre-release character biographies and premise.
  • Unreviewed content syndicated from social platforms, news aggregators or Rockstar feeds. Scheduled Newswire checks create review issues only and cannot publish directly to the website.
  • Autoplaying trailers, unrequested character/story art or promotional video thumbnails. Neutral scenic screenshots may appear in the public Newswire unless Strict Spoiler Lock is enabled.

What is allowed

Release dates, official regional times, supported platforms, pre-load instructions and other practical launch logistics, after editorial review. The news page contains intentionally brief summaries with direct citations so you can decide whether to open a source. The public Newswire includes scenic Rockstar Games screenshots accompanying neutral summaries; these images are not character biographies and disappear when Strict Spoiler Lock is enabled. The optional Rockstar-confirmed Leonida Files section contains pre-release character biographies, the official story premise and named locations, with matching promotional screenshots. It starts hidden, and its character/location images and text appear only when you choose Show Story Details. Hide Story Details clears the displayed cards; a reload also resets the reveal. The archive is only requested after you choose Show Story Details. The official screenshot browser remains hidden until you select Reveal official media; promotional images can show locations, characters and gameplay. Launch Week's optional Strict Spoiler Lock disables both the story guide and the media reveal control, and immediately closes any open guide or gallery. It is off by default; both opt-in gates remain active either way.

External links and limitations

Linked official announcements may include additional details, video, artwork or promotional media, and external content can change after we review it. We cannot guarantee external pages are spoiler-free. Link labels distinguish external destinations, which always open in a new browser tab where supported.

GTA VI cover art and the Leonida map

The homepage includes the official illustrated cover poster by default. Enabling Strict Spoiler Lock hides it and prevents a new poster image request. The Leonida poster map appears on its own page only when visitors open that destination; viewing it is optional.

After release

Our default remains spoiler-free after launch. We will not automatically switch the feed to gameplay or story content once the timer reaches zero. The archived launch presentation and shareable cards contain original sunset artwork and historical release details, not story gameplay imagery.

Report a problem

If an item is inaccurate or risks spoiling the experience, please report it on GitHub using a spoiler-free issue title. Do not include plot details in the issue itself.
