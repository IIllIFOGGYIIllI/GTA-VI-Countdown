Spoiler Policy

LAST UPDATED: 15 NOVEMBER 2026

Our priority is to protect the experience of discovering Grand Theft Auto VI for yourself. The site is spoiler-conscious by design, rather than an uncontrolled live feed.

What we do not publish

  • Leaks, datamined material, stolen footage, early gameplay uploads or rumours.
  • Story beats, missions, endings, character twists or surprise reveals.
  • Unreviewed content syndicated from social platforms, news aggregators or Rockstar feeds. Scheduled Newswire checks create review issues only and cannot publish directly to the website.
  • Autoplaying trailers, gallery screenshots or promotional video thumbnails that could reveal content unexpectedly.

What is allowed

Release dates, official regional times, supported platforms, pre-load instructions and other practical launch logistics, after editorial review. The news page contains intentionally brief summaries with direct citations so you can decide whether to open a source. The official screenshot browser is hidden until you select Reveal official media; after opting in, promotional images can show locations, characters and gameplay.

External links and limitations

Linked official announcements may include additional details, video, artwork or promotional media, and external content can change after we review it. We cannot guarantee external pages are spoiler-free. Link labels distinguish external destinations, which always open in a new browser tab where supported.

After release

Our default remains spoiler-free after launch. We will not automatically switch the feed to gameplay or story content once the timer reaches zero.

Report a problem

If an item is inaccurate or risks spoiling the experience, please report it on GitHub using a spoiler-free issue title. Do not include plot details in the issue itself.
