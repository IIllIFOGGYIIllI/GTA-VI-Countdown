01 / The Story Of The City

A City Across Generations

Choose a milestone to explore Vice City's history in release order.

Where The Name Began

The Original Grand Theft Auto

Vice City first appeared as one of three explorable cities in the original top-down Grand Theft Auto, alongside Liberty City and San Andreas. The celebrated 2002 game later reimagined the setting as a fully 3D, 1980s-inspired world.

The Strong National Museum Of Play ↗
Original Game

Grand Theft Auto: Vice City

Rockstar North took the open-world formula to a sun-soaked, Miami-inspired city. Set in 1986, the game follows Tommy Vercetti as he seeks influence in a city of nightclubs, beaches and criminal ambition.

Rockstar's Original Game Archive ↗
The Prequel

Vice City Stories

Released in 2006, this prequel moves the in-game calendar back to 1984. Players follow Vic Vance through an earlier chapter of the same 1980s-inspired city.

Explore Vice City Stories ↗
Ten Years Later

Vice City Turns Ten

Rockstar marked the tenth anniversary with a new trailer and a mobile edition for iOS and Android, sharing its neon-soaked streets with another generation.

Watch The Anniversary Trailer ↗
A New Presentation

The Definitive Edition

Vice City returned in Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy – The Definitive Edition, with updated lighting, textures, controls and navigation alongside GTA III and San Andreas.

Read Rockstar's Announcement ↗
A New Era

Vice City In GTA VI

Rockstar has confirmed that Grand Theft Auto VI returns to modern-day Vice City in the wider state of Leonida. This is a new interpretation, not a promise that individual streets or buildings are identical.

Explore Rockstar's GTA VI Site ↗

Timeline years are release milestones. 1984 and 1986 refer to the fictional settings of the two classic stories.
